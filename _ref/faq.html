QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our process, turnaround, style matching and how we work together.

01 What kind of projects can you handle? +

We work across wedding films, photo retouching and social reels. From single-day elopements to full multi-day celebration shoots, we scale to your volume.

02 How do you match our editing style? +

We start with a style brief and your reference material. After the first batch we run a detailed QC pass together, fine-tune based on your notes, and build a style guide that travels with every future project.

03 How are revisions managed? +

All revision feedback is collected through Frame.io with frame-accurate comments. You get a defined revision round per project scope — no chasing, no email threads.

04 Can you support a full wedding season? +

Yes. We're built for studios that shoot 30–100+ weddings per year. Our bulk tracking system keeps every project visible and moving.

05 What's your typical turnaround time? +

Most wedding films and photo galleries are delivered within 2-3 weeks, with rush options available for tighter deadlines. Reels and social content typically turn around within 3-5 business days.

06 How is pricing structured? +

We price per project based on volume and service mix—editing, retouching, or full post-production support. Get in touch for a custom quote tailored to your studio's workflow.

07 What file formats do you deliver? +

Final films are delivered in your preferred format (MP4, ProRes, etc.) at up to 4K resolution. Photos are delivered as high-res JPEGs or RAW-edited TIFFs, organized and ready for client galleries.

08 Is our footage and client data secure? +

Yes. All project files are handled under NDA, with access limited to your assigned editing team and transferred through encrypted, password-protected channels.

09 How do we get started? +

Book a quick onboarding call where we review your workflow, volume and style preferences. Once scoped, you can send your first project and we begin editing within days.

10 How much notice do you need? +

None at all. Just reach out, brief us on the project, and hand over the footage — we'll get started from there.

11 Will the couple notice? +

No. We work entirely in the background. The finished film goes to your studio under your brand — your clients will never know we were involved.

12 What happens if I cancel? +

If the edit hasn't started, you won't be charged. If we've already begun working through the footage, a fee applies to cover the time spent. Either way, just let us know.

13 How do I send footage? +

Google Drive or Dropbox. Share the folder link and we'll take it from there.

14 What software do you use? +

Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, CapCut, Lightroom, and Photoshop — whatever fits your project and preferred delivery format.

15 Do you work internationally? +

Yes. We work with studios across the UK, Europe, Australia, the US, and beyond. Everything is handled remotely so location is never a barrier.

16 What kinds of weddings do you edit? +

All of them. South Asian, Western, multicultural, destination — all styles, all cultures, all faiths. If you filmed it, we can cut it.
